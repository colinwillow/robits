CMB1496 - plasma orb wake: narrow and deep instead of field-wide; manta perception lag

PLASMA ORB FLIGHT WAKE
The wake radius came off a fixed constant, not off the ball: (85 + charge*70) * 2.3 = ~356 units at
full charge, against a ball whose own radius is 18. Twenty ball-widths of grid churning on every
shot, re-armed 20 times a second, which is why the whole field went crazy rather than the ball
leaving a mark.

Measured before/after (fully charged orb, flat shot, floor sheet):
  displaced nodes   345 -> 47
  median lateral    176u -> ~44u (one cell)
  footprint radius  356u -> 73u
  max Y depth       253u -> 151u over a 5x smaller area

The blocker was that power and width were the same dial: impactLocal derived its crater radius as
40 + power*10, so the only way to press deeper was to press wider. impactLocal/gridSheetsBlast now
take an options object that decouples them - opt.rad sets the crater width outright, opt.shockMul
scales (or silences) the expanding ripple, opt.noAsset keeps a per-frame travel wake out of the
heavy asset/platform deform fan-out. _gridBowl/_gridSpiral forward it and take a separate vertical
reach, so a ball flying at chest height still bites the floor without that reach dictating width.
All existing callers pass neither and are byte-for-byte unchanged.

Honest limit: the floor grid's cell is 44 units. A crater smaller than one cell can only grab a
node or two, so depth stops reading as a trench and starts reading as single-vertex spikes -
verified on screenshots, not just numbers. 73u (~1.7 cells) is the tightest that still draws as a
bowl.

MANTA RAY TRACKING
It read your transform every frame, so it rose and fell with a jump on the same frame you did.
Measured: a 0.9s hop moved it 85 of your 110 units - 77% mirror, half-follow in 0.29s.

It now flies at where it BELIEVES you are: a short dead time (it is still acting on what it saw a
beat ago), then a first-order follow toward that stale reading, with altitude lagging hardest since
that was the worst tell. The attenuation falls out for free and is the point - a fast hop barely
moves the belief before you have landed again, while standing on a ledge still brings it all the
way up.

  0.9s hop         77% mirror -> 24%
  half-follow      0.29s -> 4.61s
  sustained climb  still follows fully (85%)
  horizontal chase 4.35s -> 4.38s (untouched)

Contact and damage tests still use your true position; only where it chooses to fly is lagged.
_MANTA_LAG=0 restores instant tracking.
