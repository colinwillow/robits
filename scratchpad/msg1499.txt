CMB1499 - honour the bike's authored emissive map instead of overwriting it with the diffuse

Rev 01 shipped a diffuse texture only, so the only way to make the bike read against the neon world
was to promote the diffuse to the emissive slot wholesale - every pixel self-lit at 2.4x.

Rev 02 ships a real emissiveTexture with emissiveFactor [1,1,1]: the export says WHICH parts glow.
The old line ran anyway and assigned m.map over m.emissiveMap, so the authored glow map was
discarded on load and the whole body was flooded instead. The nozzle rings, which are what the map
actually marks, came out no hotter than the chassis.

An authored emissive map now outranks the fallback, and the diffuse is only promoted when there is
no emissive map at all - which keeps rev 01 (and every other single-texture asset) rendering
exactly as before. Emissive strength honours the export's own KHR_materials_emissive_strength when
it declares one (the 'white' material asks for 2) and otherwise lifts to 2.0.

  verified in-game: material 'hoverbike' map=yes emissiveMap=yes, emissiveMap !== map, intensity 2
  A/B screenshots at an identical camera with the wireframe twin hidden: the jet nozzle rings read
  as hot authored highlights, where the old path spread the same light flatly over the whole body
