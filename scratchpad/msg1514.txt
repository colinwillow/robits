You were right, and the specific symptom you described named the bug exactly. In a front-steer model
yaw rate is (speed/wheelbase)*tan(lock) - so REVERSING INVERTS THE STEERING. Hold the left stick
back and turn right and the two genuinely cancel. That is a faithful vehicle model and completely
the wrong one for this. It is off; the tank is the default again, along with the grip/slip and the
strafe fade I had drifted away from taming it.

  measured: reversing at full lock now turns the SAME WAY as going forward (it did not before).
  Idle turn rate 68 deg/s, matching the original tank's 67.

THE JET-SKI QUALITY IS NOW PURELY VISUAL, which is what you were describing all along.
The vehicle's logical heading, position, hitbox, guns and every control read the raw values exactly
as the tank always did. Only the DRAWN model is spring-loaded to them:

  YAW      the body leans late into a turn and OVERSHOOTS the heading coming out of one
  BANK     the drawn lean springs around the eased target, so exiting a carve over-corrects,
           corrects and settles rather than just decaying to level
  LATERAL  changes in sideways velocity throw the body sideways and it springs back - the elastic
           band flick when you stop a hard strafe or snap out of a turn
  BOB      a slight idle bob within its own space

The rider follows the DRAWN bike, not the logical one, or the spring visibly detaches him from the
machine he is sitting on. His AIM stays on the logical heading: the wobble is a look, never an
aiming error.

  measured: drawn yaw lags up to 12 deg into a turn then crosses PAST the heading coming out;
  lateral throw swings +2.5 / -2.4 units and crosses back through zero;
  aim vs logical heading differs by 0.000 deg - control is provably untouched.
