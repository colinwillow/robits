CMB1508 - hover bike: front-steer (single-track) turning, so it carves instead of pivoting

The bike was a TANK. One line rotated the whole vehicle in place at a rate the stick set directly,
with a floor under it so it spun even at a standstill. That is why it could never carve: a tank has
no front and no back, only yaw. No amount of tuning that rate produces a jet ski.

A front-steered vehicle - jet ski, snowboard, skateboard - turns because the FRONT is angled and the
body is dragged around it:

        yawRate = (speed / wheelbase) * tan(steerAngle)

Everything you described falls out of that single line. Turn RADIUS is set by how far you push the
stick and is independent of speed, while turn RATE scales with speed - so you carve an arc instead
of pivoting, the tail follows the nose through it, and rocking the stick left-right swings the back
end like a pendulum. Reversing steers the opposite way for free, because speed goes negative.

  measured, full lock, three throttle settings:
                       full        half        idle
    TANK   yaw rate    119.5deg/s  82.2deg/s   67.7deg/s     <- barely changes; spins on the spot
           radius      344         131         17
    FRONT  yaw rate    127.6deg/s  47.1deg/s   11.7deg/s     <- scales with speed, 10.9x range
           radius      314         228         157

  Idle pivoting is down 5.8x. The radius is not perfectly speed-independent because a little
  low-speed nose authority is kept deliberately (_BIKE_PIVOT) - a real jet ski cannot turn at rest,
  which is truthful and also annoying in a game. Set it to 0 for a pure carve.

Banking is now driven by the yaw rate it is ACTUALLY turning at rather than raw stick. Full lock
while barely moving is not a turn and no longer looks like one; the same lock at speed is a hard
carve and banks like it. That coupling is most of what sells the pendulum across a transition.

Knobs: _BIKE_WHEELBASE (carve radius), _BIKE_STEER_MAX (lock), _BIKE_PIVOT (standstill authority),
_BIKE_LEAN_K / _BIKE_YAW_REF (how hard it banks). _RIDE_FRONT_STEER=false restores the tank.
