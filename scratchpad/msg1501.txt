CMB1501 - bike: altitude hold while shooting, explosive knock-off, and riding as an exclusive state

ALTITUDE HOLD
The latch logic was already right - centre-hold arms it, deflecting while your finger stays down
holds, a full release drops it - but "hold" only zeroed the sink and left the climb to bleed off
through flight drag. That is a 0.38s time constant, so deflecting the stick to shoot still carried
you ~27 units higher before it settled. Holding now actively damps the vertical velocity to zero,
so the bike PARKS at the height it is at.

  measured, the exact sequence, drift over 3s each:
    finger down + centred            climbs
    finger down + deflected to fire  +27  ->  +1     (parked, still firing)
    released                         falls          (-23)
    fresh touch already deflected    keeps falling  (-11, firing) - never centred, so never latched

KNOCKED OFF BY EXPLOSIVES AND MELEE, NOT BY BULLETS
There was already a mount knock-off rule doing exactly this for creature rides - explosions always
launch, a melee launches once its lift clears the gate, plain bullets only chip - and the bike was
the one mount not covered by it, so every hit just shoved the rider around while he stayed welded
to the saddle. The bike now goes through the same rule, and dismounts silently so the knock's own
lift does the flinging. It re-parks where you came off, so you can get back on it.

  measured: bullet-weight hit -> still riding.  blast -> dismounted, pvy 368, bike re-parked.

RIDING IS AN EXCLUSIVE STATE
_mantaMount checked for _mount/_ballActive/_deathSeq but not for the bike, so flying past a manta
handed you to the manta while still on the bike. Two gates: the manta refuses a rider who is
already riding, and _nearestGrab returns nothing at all while you are on the bike - no boarding
creatures, no weapon pads, no armory. Stepping off is the EJECT button's job, not an interact.

  measured: tap-mount returns false, _grabItem returns false, and 40 frames of a manta sitting
  directly on top of the player never caught him.

NOT BUILT (deferred as requested): vehicle health, damage smoke, explode-and-shatter, regenerate.
