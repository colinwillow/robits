CMB1517 - the sun sets instead of vanishing (chunk 2)

The sun is a DOM element inside #synthwave-bg. On deploy, _voidSunSet demotes that entire layer
behind the canvas AND hands the scene an opaque gradient in the same breath, so the scene's own sky
occludes the sun in a single frame. .sw-sun has carried a 1.2s opacity/transform transition the
whole time - it simply never got the chance to run, because nothing ever changed its opacity; the
layer just went away underneath it.

body.sun-setting is now added the moment DEPLOY is pressed, which is ahead of the handoff, and it
drives the sun's opacity to 0 and sinks it down the screen. It is cleared whenever a menu comes
back, so the sun is up again for the next deploy. window._SUN_SET_FADE=false disables it.

  measured: the sun now travels down the screen (top 306 -> 787) and fades (1 -> 0.93 -> 0) instead
  of disappearing with its layer.

HONEST ABOUT WHERE THIS LANDED: it is better, not perfect. The handoff fires ~480ms after the press
(the stage paints sooner than the 1.6s backstop), so the tail of the fade is clipped - measured
0.93 still on screen when the scene sky takes over. I tried sizing the fade to fit that runway and
the transition then did not run at all (no intermediate opacity ever sampled across 20 frames),
which I could not resolve before running out of room. So: the sun visibly sets and sinks, but the
last ~7% of the fade is still a cut rather than a finish.

The clean finish is to start the fade a beat EARLIER than the deploy press, or to hold the gradient
handoff until the sun reports done, rather than racing the two. That is the next move on this one.
