CMB1503 - WILD ANIMAL brain: the manta is a neutral creature, not a hunter pointed at you

Built as a shared archetype (_wildTick / _wildProvoke), not manta-specific, so the sky dragon and
anything else free-roaming can adopt it by calling one function.

A wild animal is NEUTRAL. It is not a spawned attacker with a target; it is a thing that lives here
and is off doing its own business until something gives it a reason not to be:

  IT DOES NOT KNOW WHO THE PLAYER IS
    The enemy loop hands every creature `player`, which is exactly why the match felt pointed at
    you. Prey is now "nearest of the human and every AI bot", chosen by the creature itself.

  IT ONLY ENGAGES ON PROVOCATION
    Someone strays inside its notice radius (330), or someone hurts it. Interest then decays over
    ~11s and it goes back to wandering - no permanent grudge.

  WANDER IS A REAL STATE
    Slow drift between waypoints across the arena at its OWN altitude, chasing nobody, with the
    volleys, eye laser and dive all switched off. Still perfectly mountable while it does this - a
    wild animal you can catch a ride on, not a boss encounter.

  SHOOTING IT HAS CONSEQUENCES
    knock() records the direction the hit came from and the prey pick prefers whoever lies along
    it, so sniping it from range brings it after YOU specifically rather than the nearest bystander.

It also stopped ARRIVING at you: the ambient spawn placed it in a ring around the player, so even
a perfectly neutral creature looked like it had come for you - the entrance itself was the tell.
It now spawns against the arena centre.

TWO BUGS FOUND BY MEASURING
  · The forget radius (760) was smaller than the retaliate radius (1400), and the forget check ran
    on the same tick as acquisition - so retaliating against a shooter standing further away than
    the forget radius silently retargeted onto a nearby bystander immediately. An active
    provocation now wins outright, and the chase range can never be shorter than the range it can
    be provoked from.
  · Leaving the neutral state did not rename _state (the orbit branch never assigns it), so it
    reported 'wander' while actively hunting.

Measured in-game:
  neutral      state 'wander', no prey, distance to an idle player 900 -> 866 across the sample
               (it drifts; it does not close in)
  proximity    engages, prey = PLAYER
  lapse        returns to 'wander', prey cleared
  retaliation  shot from 922 away -> engages and targets PLAYER, not the nearer bot
  bots         confirmed valid prey - it selected a BotPilot when that was the nearest target
