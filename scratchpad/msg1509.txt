SKATE - the thing you were describing
Your own locomotion scheme, applied to something that cannot pivot. You push a world direction on
the left stick, camera-relative, exactly like on foot - and the bike goes there, except it has to
CARVE its way round rather than snapping to it. Heading error becomes steering lock and the
bicycle model does the rest, so a hard reversal swings the tail through a wide arc. Stick magnitude
is throttle, so easing the stick out is easing off.

  measured: holding a direction settles to steer=0 and heading drift 0.00 - it converges and
  tracks straight, it does not hunt. Demanding the OPPOSITE direction at speed swept the heading
  238 degrees through an arc 702 units wide. It did not pivot on the spot.
