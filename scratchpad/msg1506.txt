CMB1506 - MECH LIGHTNING DRAGON: the manta's brain, a new body, nothing rebuilt

You asked whether it could just be APPLIED rather than reimplemented. It can, and the reason is that
none of what makes the manta work is manta-specific: the neutral wander, the provoke rules, prey
selection across the human and every bot, the standoff orbit, the dive and its sting, eye bolts, the
charge-up laser, knockback, the ride/hang offsets - all of that lives in MantaRay and _wildTick.

The hinge is _rig.group. That group is the object every one of those systems rotates, so the GLB is
parented INSIDE it and the procedural manta meshes are simply hidden. The dragon inherits the entire
flight rig for free - bank, tanh-saturated roll and spin, dive pitch, mount offsets - and MechDragon
only handles what is genuinely new: fitting the model, its clips, and its materials. It is ~90 lines,
not a second creature.

ONE BUG WORTH RECORDING
Box3.setFromObject on a SKINNED mesh measures an unposed bind skeleton, and here it came back wrong
by two orders of magnitude - the "fitted" dragon rendered about one unit long. The fit now measures
bind-pose vertex extents times each mesh's own accumulated scale, which cannot be surprised by
skinning. (Raw span really is 0.001; you were right that it came in small.)

Orientation is derived, not guessed: the export's long axis is +X and Bip001_Head sits at POSITIVE
x, so the -90deg yaw maps the head onto the rig's +Z forward - it flies head-first.

NOT DONE: mounting/hanging on it (the 'ride' bone is found and waiting), and any fire breath. Its
inherited attacks are the manta's - eye bolts, the tracking laser, and a diving sting.

Caveat: I verified this functionally and by measurement, but could not frame a clean close-up in the
headless harness. Glow level, yaw and size are all live knobs (_DRAGON_EGLOW / _DRAGON_YAW /
_DRAGON_SPAN) so you can tune it on the phone without a rebuild.
