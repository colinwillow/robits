CMB1513 - kill the giant white lattice laid over the ground during the drop

It is the STAGE PORTAL's energy-field visualiser - the spoke-mandala reused from zone -17, sitting in
the portal lens. Two facts about it combine badly:

  · it is authored at ROOM scale (outer radius ~2580, geometry bbox +/-4721) and is only brought down
    to lens size by a per-frame fit inside _stagePortalTick;
  · it is frustumCulled=false, renderOrder 2, additive - so at that raw scale it paints across the
    whole screen from anywhere in the world, over everything.

And the reason it appears exactly during the fall and vanishes exactly on touchdown: THE ZONE UPDATE
IS PAUSED for the whole drop - only the ship's own visuals are ticked - so _stagePortalTick stops
running and whatever it last wrote just persists. Landing tears the stage down, which is what
"removes" it.

That last part is why my first fix did nothing. Gating visibility inside the portal tick is
unreachable code during a drop. It has to be switched off from the drop side, where code is
actually running.

Two changes:
  1. the mesh is BORN HIDDEN and only turned on by the tick once it has actually been fitted to the
     lens - nothing may draw it before it has been sized;
  2. _dropStart switches it off explicitly.

  measured: mid-fall, a scan for any visible LineSegments with radius 2000-4000 and opacity >0.5
  went from finding it (8544 verts, white, op 0.95, renderOrder 2) to "not found".
  On the stage before the drop it is still visible:true - the portal is unchanged in normal use.

Found by trapping Object3D.add and reading the construction stack, after four rounds of guessing
from geometry measurements got nowhere. Worth remembering: for "what IS this object", trap where it
is created rather than trying to identify it from its shape.
