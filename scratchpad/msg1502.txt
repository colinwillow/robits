CMB1502 - bike damage loop: the vehicle takes the hits, smokes, detonates, and rebuilds

You are riding a machine, so the machine is what gets shot. Incoming damage goes into the bike's
own health instead of yours - the same shape as ball mode shielding you - and the bike visibly
fails under fire. This is not a way to be invulnerable: explosives and melee still launch you off
directly through the mount knock-off rule, and the bike dying is itself what throws you clear.

  HEALTH   45 (_BIKE_MAX_HP). Absorbs all incoming damage while it lives; damage numbers show in
           the vehicle's colour so you can tell whose health bar just moved.
  SMOKE    from 55% health, ramping to zero: a grey plume off the tail that inherits the bike's
           motion, plus ember spit below 30%. A health bar you read without looking at one.
  DEATH    vox fireball + a chassis shard burst + debris + a grid crater + the large boom, and the
           rider is launched with blast:true so the knock-off rule flings him for certain.
  REBUILD  after 14s at the spot the LEVEL parked it (not where it died), with a materialise pop.

ONE BUG WORTH RECORDING
hp and maxHp were initialised at two separate call sites, and the arena teardown - which runs
BEFORE the build that parks the bike - set hp from maxHp||45, leaving hp=45 with maxHp undefined.
The later `if(hp==null)` init then never fired. Every health ratio read NaN, and NaN<=0 is false,
so the bike could absorb unlimited damage and never explode. Caught by the probe, not by reading.
_bikeHpInit now owns the pair and no caller can set half of it.

Measured in-game:
  absorb    player took 0, bike took 6 (45 -> 39)
  smoke     live grey particles near the bike: 0 healthy, 14 at 35%, 39 at 12% (first tuning
            peaked at 6 and was invisible on screen - confirmed on a screenshot both times)
  death     dead, dismounted, wreck hidden, rider launched at pvy 499
  rebuild   returns at its home spot (180,-240) with 45/45
