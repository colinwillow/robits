CMB1497 - hover bike: rider lean, handlebar clips, particle jets, arena spawn, real flight

RIDER LEANED HARDER THAN THE BIKE
Two causes in the same line. Player.draw defaulted _BIKE_BANK to 1.5 while _bikeTick defaults the
same knob to 1.0, so the rider banked 50% further than the machine he is sitting on; and draw read
the RAW per-frame lean while the bike eases toward its bank, so the rider also swung out FIRST.
The rider's roll is now written from the bike's own settled bank and scaled to a fraction of it
(_BIKE_RIDER_BANK, 0.55). Written inside _bikeTick, which is the frame's LAST writer - draw runs
earlier, so anything derived there is a frame stale and could exceed the bike around a zero
crossing. The seat-arc offset reads the same value instead of recomputing its own.

  measured: bike roll 21.5deg, rider 11.8deg, ratio 0.550-0.551 every sample, never exceeds

HANDLEBARS SLID ON A LEFT TURN
This one is in the export, not the blend. In models/hoverbike.glb the 'right' clip translates
handle_bars by [0,0,0] and rotates it -14.8deg; 'left' rotates the exact mirror (+14.8) but also
carries a position track of [12.8,-21.1,-2.1]. The rotations are authored correctly - only the
left clip's baked position is asymmetric.

Rather than require a re-export, a steering pose is now enforced to be ROTATION ONLY: every turn
clip's position/scale tracks are pinned to the neutral pose's values and only quaternion tracks
blend, so asymmetric authoring cannot leak into the pose at all. 70 values pinned on load.

  measured after: LEFT and RIGHT handle_bars position identical, quaternions exact mirrors

JET EXHAUST
The boost FX was one cone mesh that swivelled and scaled. A cone is a shape, not exhaust. All four
jet cans now emit real particle streams whose DIRECTION is the thrust reaction: out the BACK under
throttle, straight DOWN when climbing, and the normalised blend while doing both.

  measured: particles within 90u of the bike - 7 idle, 29 on throttle, 57 on lift; confirmed on
  screenshots (plume trails behind under throttle, fires downward under lift)

A BIKE YOU CAN ACTUALLY REACH
It only ever existed inside the circuit zone. One is now parked beside the armory terminal in the
platform arena - walk up, same right-stick tap-to-mount as every other interact, and it re-parks
wherever you step off. Leaves with the arena unless you are riding it.

FLYING MOTORCYCLE
The hover model pulled height back to the cushion with a spring (-hopY*K): the higher you climbed
the harder it yanked you down, so it could never be more than a hover by construction. Flight mode
removes the spring entirely - the jet is the only lift, and letting go leaves a gentle constant
sink with drag, so you settle rather than drop. Lift also drops from 1250 to 620, because the old
figure was sized to fight a spring that no longer exists and just launched you.

  measured: climb to 128 and rising (ceiling 2600), 27u of coast after release (200u before the
  lift/drag retune), then a steady ~45/s settle

Note on the control: in the ghost scheme lift is press-and-hold the CENTRE of the right stick -
right-stick UP fires the bike guns. Say the word if you want lift moved onto up.
