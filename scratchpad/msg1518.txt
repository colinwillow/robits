CMB1518 - the machine hands over instead of leaving a hole (chunk 3)

The forge's quantum unit was cleared on a fixed 120ms timer after the deploy press, but its
replacement - the freestanding _stageJack - is not built until zone -32 is up, hundreds of
milliseconds later. So the machine popped out of existence and a new one popped back in, which is
exactly what your screenshots 1->2 and 3->4 show.

That 120ms timer was itself a fix for the OPPOSITE bug: for a while both machines were on screen at
once at different scales. A timer cannot solve either problem, because the thing it has to be
sequenced against is a zone build of unknown duration - too short and you get a gap, too long and
you get a double. So it is not a timer any more: the forge unit is retired in the exact statement
its replacement comes into being. No gap, and no two-at-once either, by construction rather than by
timing. The old timer survives only as a long backstop for a route that never reaches the deploy
room at all.
