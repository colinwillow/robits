CMB1504 - bike/rider orientation: pitch coupling, a launch that tumbles, and a bike that floats down

RIDER STAYED UPRIGHT WHILE THE BIKE STOOD ON ITS NOSE
CMB1497 coupled the rider's ROLL to the bike and stopped there, so nothing tied the pitch axis
together at all. Flight made that obvious: the nose-pitch term reads vertical speed through a
coefficient (0.0016) sized for hover-era velocities, and flight terminal is ~186/s - which pinned
it at the +/-0.3 clamp for the whole climb AND the whole fall. So the bike sat at maximum nose
deflection, flipping sign between climbing and falling, while the rider was held at rotation.x=0.
That is the "bike tilts one way, character the other" in both screenshots.

Two fixes: the rider now takes the bike's pitch (scaled, like the roll - a rider leans less than his
machine on every axis), written from _bikeTick which is the frame's last writer; and the carve
coefficient is softened in flight so the nose reads the actual climb rate instead of saturating.

  measured  climbing  bike 10.7deg / rider 0deg  ->  bike 6.2 / rider 5.0
            falling   bike  3.1deg / rider 0deg  ->  bike -1.9 / rider -1.5

"HE DISAPPEARS AND REAPPEARS" WAS 520 OF LIFT
He was never hidden - measured visible on every frame. The blast threw him ~440 units up at ~500/s,
which outruns the chase camera's own easing, so he left the top of the frame and came back a second
later. Lift cut to 285; apex is now ~269 and he stays on screen (confirmed on a screenshot).

A LAUNCH NOW TUMBLES INSTEAD OF LYING DOWN
The knockback pose holds the body RIGID along the flight line. Rigid plus horizontal reads as
"lying down in mid-air", not "thrown" - exactly what the screenshot showed. It now rolls steadily
about its own flight axis, spinning down as the launch decays so the landing still resolves through
the existing exit blend. This affects every player launch, not just the bike. _KB_TUMBLE=false
restores the rigid pose.

A BIKE LEFT IN MID-AIR SETTLES
Stepping off at altitude re-parked it at ground level, so it teleported to the floor under you. It
now parks at the height it was actually left at and sinks from there at its own flight rate - it is
anti-grav, it does not fall.

  measured  ejected at height 67 -> bike parks at 51 and glides 53 -> 32 -> 16 -> -7 -> rest
