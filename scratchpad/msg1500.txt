CMB1500 - bike: fall at 54% of climb speed, and a gun that hits like a vehicle's

FALL SPEED
SINK and LIFT set both terminal speeds and they are coupled: climb=(LIFT-SINK)/DRAG,
fall=SINK/DRAG. The first flight pass (135/620/2.6) gave 186 up but only 52 down - a 28% ratio,
which is the "falls way too slow". Raising SINK alone would have eaten the climb with it, so both
moved: 312/795/2.6.

  measured in-game: climb 21/s, fall 11/s -> 54% (was 28%)

THE GUN WAS WEAKER THAN THE RIFLE YOU RODE UP ON
At 2.5 damage it barely beat a plain laser round (2), so dismounting really was the better play.
But the deeper reason it felt like a nudge is that the bullet->enemy path hard-coded forceMul=0.1
for EVERY round: force = 0.85*dmg*vuln*forceMul, so a vehicle cannon and a pistol round shoved
identically no matter what damage they carried. Damage alone could never have fixed it.

Rounds can now carry their own weight - _kbMul, _kbLift, _heavy - and unset, every existing bullet
keeps the exact old 0.1 nudge. The bike's bolts declare all three: 8 damage, 0.5 knock multiplier,
90 lift, heavy 3.

Spiders also took a FIXED shove (_SPIDER_SHOVE) regardless of what hit them; that now scales with
the round's weight too.

  measured, same spider (hp 20), old gun vs new:
    peak shove   95.4  ->  296.1   (3.1x)
    peak lift       0  ->  295     (off its feet, was none)
    hp per burst 12.5  ->  16

  The damage ratio per bolt is 3.2x but burst damage only rose 1.3x, because the heavier knockback
  shoves the target out of the firing line - sustained DPS on one target does NOT scale with the
  damage number. That is the trade the knockback buys, not a bug.

BULLETS DEFORM THE GRID
_heavy scales the sheet impact, the travel wake and the tint radius, and a heavy round now punches
a real crater through gridSheetsBlast. The crater is deliberately narrow (opt.rad ~ one grid cell,
using the decoupling added in CMB1496): the plasma-orb lesson is that width is what turns a hit
into a field-wide smear, so the weight goes into depth.

  measured, same 2.2s burst: max grid displacement 25.1 -> 75.2 (3x deeper) on 15 -> 17 nodes
  (same footprint, three times the depth)
