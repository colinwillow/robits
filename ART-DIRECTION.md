# ROBITS — ART DIRECTION (the two-screen test)

**The canon references are the START screen (DJ robot on the hoverboard: turntable, twin
speakers, striped sun, perspective grid) and MISSION CONTROL (mandala square-chain
visualizer over the sunset).** Every asset — particle, building, machine, world, planet,
UI chip — must pass one question: *would it sit comfortably inside those two frames?*
If the hues, shading, or silhouette would clash there, redo it before shipping.

## Palette (sample from the screens, don't drift)
- Base: deep indigo/violet `#1a1038` → near-black `#0d0820`; dusk sky gradient into sunset
- Sunset core: warm gold `#ffb03a` → hot orange `#ff7a3a` → magenta `#ff2d95`
- Neon set: mint/teal `#3dead0`, cyan `#33e0ff`, lavender/violet `#8a6cff`, hot pink `#ff2d95`
- Accents: warm orange dots (`#ff9a3a`) as the "eye" inside cool shapes (speaker cones, sun)
- Text/UI: pale mint or lavender on translucent dark chips with thin neon borders

## Shading — the actual signature
- **SOLID, EVEN FILLS.** Flat or near-flat shading, two tones per surface max.
  No photoreal gradients, no HDR wash, no blown-out whites — evenness is the look.
- **Glow is a halo, not a flood.** Neon rims and soft outer halos around silhouettes;
  the screen itself never bleaches. If bloom is eating a shape, the source is too bright.
- Chunky, rounded, friendly silhouettes; visible edge lines; quads and square grids only
  (never triangle diagonals).

## Motifs (reuse these, they're the brand)
- Music tech: speakers, turntables, EQ bars, waveforms, records — machines feel like
  audio gear even when they're weapons or buildings.
- Hovering things with soft thruster sparkle trails (the hoverboard, the speaker stacks).
- The striped sun; perspective neon grids meeting a horizon; scanline stripes.
- Mandala / square-chain particle choreography (Mission Control) for any visualizer,
  portal, or energy effect.
- Symmetric composition: one hero object centered, flanked pairs, dark corner vignette.

## Application notes
- Worlds/planets: land = solid banded fills in the palette; ocean = deep indigo→teal;
  emissives limited to seams, crests, windows, beacons — small areas, strong color.
- Particles: choose 2–3 palette colors per system, never rainbow-random.
- UI: dark translucent chips, 1.5px neon borders, letterspaced Orbitron, mint/lavender text.
- When a scene reads washed/white/hazy on device: reduce source brightness and area
  first (per "glow is a halo"), never crank exposure.
